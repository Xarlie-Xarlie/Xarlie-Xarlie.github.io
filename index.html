What I'm building now at Isaac

An event-driven platform for financial operations and payments. Most of my time goes to the Go backend.

Where I'm pushing

  • Financial writes that stay correct under concurrency and retries: unique-index collision handling, polling instead of fixed sleeps.

  • More rigor on tests and contracts: integration suites and shared API collections.
